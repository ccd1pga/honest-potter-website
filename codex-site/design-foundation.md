# Design Foundation

This note records the agreed visual foundation for the next Honest Potter design
phase. It is local design direction until the work is deliberately folded into
the live site.

## Colour Palette: Kiln Hearth

- Background: `#EEE7DC`
- Surface: `#FFFAF1`
- Text: `#22201D`
- Muted text: `#665D53`
- Brand: `#7B3F32`
- Brand dark: `#4B2922`
- Brand ink: `#FFF4E8`
- Accent: `#526A61`

The palette should feel warm, readable, restrained, and studio-grounded. The
brick colour is punctuation, not wallpaper. Let photos and ceramic surfaces
carry plenty of the colour on real pages.

## Typography Direction

Use a mixed but deliberate type system:

- Workshop Slab for the site identity and major display moments.
- Oldstyle Studio for longer reading areas, especially Studio Notes from
  subheadings onward.
- Plain readable sans-serif for navigation, buttons, captions, labels, and
  ordinary interface text.

The slab should act like a studio stamp: memorable and characterful, but not
pressed onto every small heading or label. Studio Notes should belong to the
same site while giving longer text a calmer reading rhythm.

## Logo / Maker's Mark

The existing logo is the maker's mark and should stay recognisable as that mark.
For the site, create a more deliberate digital version next: still honest to the
real pottery stamp, but better suited to navigation, footer use, favicons, and
small responsive layouts.
